# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a place where people can work together without fear of harassment.

## Our standard

- Treat others with respect. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Keep discussions on the project. Criticism of code and design is welcome; personal attacks,
  insults and discrimination of any kind are not.
- Do not harass anyone, in public or in private: no unwelcome attention, no threats, no sharing of
  other people's private information.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people who write in a language that is not their first.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces – the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions –
and to people representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and
may restrict participation of someone who repeatedly or seriously breaks it. To report a problem,
contact one of the maintainers privately; the address in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) reaches them
too. Reports are handled confidentially. People who report in good faith will not be disadvantaged.
